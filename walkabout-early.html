What a mate would ask before buying one

Does a 4-piece rod fish as well as a 1-piece?

My best fly rods are 4-piece and sell for close to $2,000. Piece count isn’t the problem. Build quality is.

What will it fish?

Barra, saratoga, jewfish, queenfish, taimen, black bass, peacock bass and more. It’s not for marlin or bream.

Do the tips really swap?

Yes. The tips share one guide setup across the spin and the baitcast.

How heavy is it?

About 125 g.

How long is it packed down?

65 cm.

What’s postage?

Australia: free for Founding rods. Otherwise $25, or $30 Express.
New Zealand: $45.
Papua New Guinea: $75.
Rest of the world: $80. The buyer pays any import taxes.

When does it ship?

The rods are in Darwin and every one is checked before it ships. Orders in by midnight Tuesday 1 December post by Thursday 3 December. After that, orders post between 8 and 10 December. Order by Monday 7 December for Parcel Post before Christmas.

How does the warranty work?

Register the Purchase ID card from the box on the Barefoot website. Registered owners also get the Fuji guide codes for spare guides.
