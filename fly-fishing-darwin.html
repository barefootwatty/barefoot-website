Tropical fly fishing is different

Fly fishing up north is different from any trout stream or lake you've set foot on. It calls for a few different skills, different setups — and very different flies.

Life revolves around the moon and tides up here. It's the number one factor in your chances of success each day you fish.

"Up here the fish are big and angry — and we don't wear tweed."— Watty

When to come

Three fly seasons in one Top End

You can fly fish all year round in the Top End, but the best of it runs from mid February to the end of November — and what you'll be casting at changes with the water.

Run-off · Mid Feb – End May

Fishing the big rivers and the colour changes as the floodwaters drain — blind casting, not sight casting, for big barra and king threadfin.

Dry season · Early Jun – End Aug

Cooler temps and clean water — shallow-water sight casting at its best, plus the freshwater billabongs.

Build-up · Early Sep – End Nov

Sight casting and the freshwater billabongs again, in warmer conditions — the water's alive before the wet arrives.

Darwin Harbour

World-class fly fishing on Darwin's doorstep

Darwin Harbour has everything a tropical fly fisher looks for. When you get the tides right and know where to look, it's all there waiting for you — minutes from town.

Bynoe Harbour

Sight-casting barra — and whatever else you're dreaming of

Saltwater fly fishing for barramundi in Bynoe Harbour is the headline act — hard-fighting fish, accurate casts in challenging tidal conditions, and insider techniques to land more fish on the fly. It's the water where Peachy and Starlo joined Watty to do exactly that.

Depending on your goals, Watty creates a personalised fishing plan to suit your desires, your abilities and — of course — the tides. Sight-cast at cruising barra all trip, chase big pelagics all week, slide gurglers over the lily pads for saratoga, or do a bit of everything.

Guests fish a high-quality catch-and-release adventure in pristine wilderness. The main species: barramundi, mangrove jack, threadfin salmon, saratoga, golden snapper, queenfish, giant trevally and Spanish mackerel — plus about forty other tropical estuary and reef fish.

Freshwater

Top End saratoga on fly

Saratoga are without doubt one of the best fish to target with fly tackle. They love crunching surface flies first thing in the morning, and they'll eat a subsurface presentation as the day goes on.

How a safari runs

Your fly fishing itinerary

Trips of 3–7 days. Depending on the length of your trip you can fish up to four different areas — Darwin Harbour, Corroboree Billabong, Dundee Beach and Bynoe Harbour.

Fishing days

Days begin as the sun comes up, about 6:30am — breakfast at your lodgings or on the boat. Then it's tides, flats, billabongs or bluewater as the plan dictates.

Knock-off

You're finished fishing and off the water around 4pm each day — unless you nominate to pull up earlier.

Built around you

A detailed itinerary is confirmed with you through the booking process, so the trip suits your needs, your casting and your goals.
