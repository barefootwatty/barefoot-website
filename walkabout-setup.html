Barefoot Rods  /  Owners

Two dots. One quarter turn.

Put your Walkabout together the same way every time and the joints wear in like a key. They go together and come apart easily, and stay put while you fish.

Watty's 30-second how-to video is coming soon.
Until then, follow the steps below.

Line up the dots

Hold one section with its dot at 12 o'clock and the other with its dot at 3 o'clock.

Push and twist

Push them together with a quarter turn until the dots meet and the joint comes up firm.

Pulling apart

Same twist, in reverse: a quarter turn back as you pull.

Hot tip

Run the joint through your hair, or rub on a little candle wax, before you put it together. It eases the joints together and apart.

Look after your rod

  • Wipe sand and grit off the joints before you put it together.
  • After saltwater, run freshwater over the rod, guides and reel seat, then let it dry.
  • Pack it dry in its bag: each section in its own sleeve, the spare tip in its tube.
  • Check the guides for cracks or grooves now and then.

Register your rod

Registered original owners can buy replacement sections and get the Fuji guide codes.
