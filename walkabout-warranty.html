Barefoot Rods  /  Owners

Register your Walkabout

Register your rod with the Purchase ID on your warranty card. As the registered original owner (including if the rod was a gift to you), you can buy replacement sections for life and get the Fuji guide codes. Rods bought or given second-hand aren't covered.

Own both? Register each one separately.

Your details are only used for your warranty. How we handle your details.

Broke a section?

It happens. Spare sections for every part of the rod are kept in Darwin, so you don't need a new rod. Registered original owners can buy a replacement for any section, posted from Darwin. Email glenn@barefootfishingsafaris.com.au and tell me which rod and which section.
